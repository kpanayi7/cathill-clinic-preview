Four therapies, one front door

Most clinics offer one answer to every problem. We keep four under the same roof, so the recommendation you get is about what your body needs — and therapies can work together when that helps you recover faster.

Chiropractic

with Dr Jenny Donovan

The clinic's founding discipline. Gentle, precise care for spines, joints and the problems they cause elsewhere — headaches, sciatica, postural pain. Led by Dr Jenny Donovan, with particular expertise in pregnancy and paediatric care.
