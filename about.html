Established in East Barnet since 1997

Cat Hill Clinic opened as a chiropractic practice in 1997. Physiotherapy, therapeutic massage and acupuncture have since been added, so that patients receive the most appropriate treatment for their condition within a single, coordinated clinic.

Our approach

Individual care, delivered to professional standards

A family practice

We care for patients of all ages, and many of our patients have been referred to us through personal recommendation by family and friends.

Honest recommendations

With four disciplines under one roof, we recommend the treatment that best meets your requirements. Where a patient would be better served elsewhere, we say so and refer appropriately.

Professional standards

Our chiropractic care is provided by a registrant of the General Chiropractic Council and member of the British Chiropractic Association. The clinic is run to the highest professional and ethical standards.

Our practitioners

Meet the team

Dr Jenny Donovan BSc(Hons) DC

Doctor of Chiropractic · Clinic Principal

Dr Donovan graduated with first-class honours from the Welsh Institute of Chiropractic in 2001, joined Cat Hill Clinic in 2002 and has been Clinic Principal since 2008. She practises a range of gentle techniques, including spinal adjustment, soft-tissue therapy and postural and exercise advice, and has a particular interest in care during pregnancy and for children.

Registered with the General Chiropractic Council · British Chiropractic Association member

James Galea

Physiotherapist · Elements Physiotherapy

James qualified from the University of Hertfordshire in 2006 and has worked in the NHS, in private practice and with sports teams. He joined Cat Hill Clinic in 2019, where his Elements Physiotherapy practice treats musculoskeletal pain, sports injuries and pre- and post-operative rehabilitation. He accepts consultant referrals and private health insurance.

Tracy Ward ITEC IIHHT

Therapeutic Massage Therapist

Tracy qualified in anatomy, physiology and body massage in 1997, and later added aromatherapy and Indian head massage to her training. She has practised at Cat Hill Clinic for many years, bringing a calm and thorough approach to muscular tension, stress and recovery.
