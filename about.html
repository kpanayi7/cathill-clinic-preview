A local clinic, nearly thirty years in the making

Cat Hill Clinic opened as a chiropractic practice in 1997. Over the years we've added physiotherapy, therapeutic massage and acupuncture — not to be everything to everyone, but so your care doesn't stop at the edge of one discipline.

What we believe

Care that fits the person, not the timetable

A family practice

We see people of every age — children, athletes, office workers, mums-to-be, grandparents. Many of our patients arrive because someone in their family already comes here.

Honest recommendations

With four therapies in one building, we recommend the treatment that fits your problem — and if what you need isn't something we offer, we'll say so.

Professional standards

Our chiropractic care is delivered by a General Chiropractic Council registrant and British Chiropractic Association member, in a clinic run to professional and ethical standards.

Meet the team

The people you'll see

Dr Jenny Donovan BSc(Hons) DC

Doctor of Chiropractic · Clinic Principal

Jenny graduated with first-class honours from the Welsh Institute of Chiropractic in 2001, joined Cat Hill in 2002 and has run the clinic since 2008. She works with a wide range of gentle techniques — spinal adjustment, soft-tissue work, posture and exercise advice — and has a particular interest in care during pregnancy and for children. After more than two decades here, she knows half of East Barnet by name.

Registered with the General Chiropractic Council · British Chiropractic Association member

James Galea

Physiotherapist · Elements Physiotherapy

James qualified from the University of Hertfordshire in 2006 and has worked across the NHS, private practice and sports teams. He joined Cat Hill in 2019, where his Elements Physiotherapy practice treats aches and pains, sports injuries, and pre- and post-surgery rehabilitation — with patients arriving by word of mouth, consultant referral and private health insurance.

Tracy Ward ITEC IIHHT

Therapeutic Massage Therapist

Tracy qualified in 1997 in anatomy, physiology and body massage, later adding aromatherapy and Indian head massage to her training. She has been treating clients at Cat Hill for years, bringing a calm, thorough approach to muscle tension, stress and recovery work.
