What physiotherapy helps with

Physiotherapy is about restoring movement and strength — after injury, after surgery, or after a problem has crept up on you gradually. Where chiropractic focuses on how joints move, physiotherapy builds the strength and control that stops the problem coming back.

  • Sports injuries — from weekend football to marathon training
  • Muscle and tendon problems: strains, tendinopathy, overuse
  • Rehabilitation before and after surgery
  • Persistent aches that haven't responded to rest
  • Insurance and consultant referrals handled routinely

How James works

James qualified from the University of Hertfordshire in 2006 and has worked in the NHS, private practice and with sports teams. Expect a proper assessment, hands-on treatment where it helps, and — the part that actually fixes you — a progressive exercise plan you can realistically follow at home, reviewed and adjusted each visit.

Your first visit

Bring shorts or leggings if the problem is in your lower body. James will assess how you move, agree goals with you ("get back to five-a-side by September" beats "feel a bit better"), and start treatment the same day where appropriate.

Common questions

Physiotherapy FAQs

Can I claim through my private health insurance?
Usually, yes — Elements Physiotherapy regularly works with private health insurers and consultant referrals. Check your policy's requirements before your first visit.
Do I need a scan or diagnosis first?
No. A physiotherapy assessment is often the fastest way to find out what's wrong — and James will tell you plainly if imaging or a GP visit is the right next step instead.
Will I have to do exercises at home?
Almost certainly — that's where most of the progress happens. The plan is built around what you'll actually do: a few minutes of the right exercises beats an hour of the perfect ones you'll skip.
