Physiotherapy

Structured, goal-led rehabilitation with James Galea of Elements Physiotherapy, practising at Cat Hill Clinic since 2019.

What physiotherapy helps with

Physiotherapy restores movement and strength following injury or surgery, or where a condition has developed gradually over time. Where chiropractic addresses the movement of the joints, physiotherapy builds the strength and control that prevents the problem from recurring.

  • Sports injuries — from weekend football to marathon training
  • Muscle and tendon problems: strains, tendinopathy, overuse
  • Rehabilitation before and after surgery
  • Persistent aches that haven't responded to rest
  • Insurance and consultant referrals handled routinely

James's approach

James qualified from the University of Hertfordshire in 2006 and has worked in the NHS, in private practice and with sports teams. Treatment begins with a thorough assessment and combines hands-on therapy, where beneficial, with a progressive exercise programme designed to be followed at home and reviewed at each visit.

Your first visit

Please bring shorts or leggings if your condition affects the lower body. James will assess your movement, agree specific goals with you and, where appropriate, begin treatment at the first appointment.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I claim through private health insurance?
Yes. Elements Physiotherapy works with most private health insurers and accepts consultant referrals. Please check your policy for any pre-authorisation requirements before your first appointment.
Do I need a scan or diagnosis first?
No. A physiotherapy assessment is usually the right first step, and in most cases imaging is not required. If a scan or specialist opinion would be helpful, James will advise you.
Will I need to do exercises at home?
In most cases, yes. A home programme is central to lasting recovery. It is designed to be realistic and is reviewed and adjusted at each visit.
