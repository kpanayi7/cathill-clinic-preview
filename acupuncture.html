Acupuncture

An evidence-informed treatment for persistent pain and general wellbeing, offered on its own or alongside your other care at the clinic.

What acupuncture helps with

Acupuncture uses very fine needles placed at specific points to stimulate the body's own responses. Patients most commonly seek treatment for long-standing pain that has not responded to other approaches, or for general wellbeing and relief from stress and fatigue.

  • Persistent and recurring pain
  • Tension-type headaches
  • Muscle tightness that keeps returning
  • Stress and general wellbeing
  • Support alongside chiropractic or massage treatment

What to expect

Treatment is considerably gentler than most people expect. The needles are extremely fine, quite unlike an injection, and most patients feel only a brief, dull sensation. Sessions are quiet and unhurried, and many patients find them deeply relaxing.

Part of a coordinated plan

At Cat Hill Clinic, acupuncture is often used alongside the other therapies, easing persistent pain while chiropractic or physiotherapy addresses the underlying mechanical cause. At your first assessment we will give you a clear and honest view of what acupuncture can offer for your particular condition.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is acupuncture painful?
Rarely. The needles are extremely fine and most patients feel only a brief, dull sensation on insertion. Any discomfort is minimal and short-lived.
Are the needles safe?
Yes. All needles are sterile, single-use and disposed of after each treatment, in line with professional standards.
How many sessions will I need?
Many patients notice a change within the first few sessions, though long-standing conditions may take longer. A realistic course will be discussed at your first assessment.
