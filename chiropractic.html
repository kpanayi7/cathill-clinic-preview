Chiropractic

The clinic's founding discipline since 1997 — gentle, precise care for spines and joints, delivered by Dr Jenny Donovan.

What chiropractic helps with

Chiropractic focuses on how your spine and joints move — and what happens when they don't. Problems here rarely stay put: a stiff segment in your lower back becomes sciatica; a locked neck becomes headaches. Chiropractic care finds the source rather than chasing the symptom.

  • Low back pain and sciatica
  • Neck pain, stiffness and tension headaches
  • Postural problems from desk work
  • Joint pain in hips, shoulders and knees
  • Care during pregnancy and for children — a particular interest of Dr Donovan's

How Jenny works

Dr Donovan uses a range of gentle techniques — precise spinal adjustments, soft-tissue and massage work, and practical advice on posture and exercise — adapted to your age, build and comfort. Treatment for a pregnant patient looks nothing like treatment for a rugby player, and both look nothing like care for a seven-year-old.

Your first visit

Allow about 45 minutes. We start with your history, examine how you move, and explain in plain English what we've found and what we recommend — including how many sessions we realistically expect you to need. If chiropractic isn't the right tool for your problem, we'll say so and point you to what is, often just down the corridor.

Common questions

Chiropractic FAQs

Does the adjustment hurt?
Most patients describe it as pressure followed by relief, not pain. Jenny uses gentle, low-force techniques where appropriate, and always explains what she's about to do before doing it.
What's the clicking sound?
Just gas releasing within the joint as it moves — the same as cracking your knuckles. It's not bones moving against each other, and an adjustment can be effective with no sound at all.
How many sessions will I need?
It depends on the problem and how long you've had it. You'll get an honest estimate at your first assessment — and if things change, the plan changes with it.
Is chiropractic safe during pregnancy?
Yes, with appropriately adapted techniques — it's one of Dr Donovan's particular interests. Every technique is adjusted for the stage of pregnancy and your comfort.
