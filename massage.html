Therapeutic Massage

Targeted soft-tissue treatment with Tracy Ward for muscular tension, overuse and stress.

What therapeutic massage helps with

Therapeutic massage treats specific muscles with specific intent: releasing tension that has built up over time, helping tissue recover after exertion, and interrupting the cycle of stress, tightness and pain that sedentary work so often produces.

  • Muscle tension in the neck, shoulders and back
  • Stress-related tightness and tension headaches
  • Recovery from sport and heavy training
  • Aromatherapy massage for deeper relaxation
  • Indian head massage

Tracy's approach

Tracy qualified in anatomy, physiology and body massage in 1997, later adding aromatherapy (IIHHT) and Indian head massage (ITEC) to her training. Each session begins with a discussion of what you need, whether focused work on a specific area or a full-body treatment, and pressure is adjusted to the individual rather than to a set routine.

Alongside the clinic's other therapies

Massage is frequently combined with chiropractic care at Cat Hill Clinic: relaxed soft tissue allows adjustments to hold better and progress to be made more quickly. Where a condition would benefit from chiropractic or physiotherapy assessment, Tracy will refer you to a colleague within the clinic.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How firm is the pressure?
As firm as you need it to be. Tracy will discuss your preference before each session and adjust throughout. Deeper work may feel intense at the time but should never be painful.
Should I choose massage or chiropractic?
As a general guide, tightness that feels muscular is well suited to massage, while restricted joints or pain that travels are better assessed by a chiropractor. If you are unsure, message the clinic or ask at reception and we will advise you.
How often should I attend?
For a specific problem, weekly sessions for a short period are common, followed by longer intervals. Many patients attend monthly for maintenance. Tracy will recommend a schedule based on your needs.
